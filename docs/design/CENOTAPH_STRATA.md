# Cenotaph Strata

This is the authoritative identity index for the seven Strata. It collects the
existing Stratum definitions so the gameplay reset has a stable reference. The
full authoring template remains in [`CONTENT_GUIDE.md`](CONTENT_GUIDE.md).

## 1. Ash-Walk

- Theme: collapse, ash, and first survival.
- Visual identity: buried roads, broken shrines, ash fields, collapsed
  settlements, low ruins, and distant vertical landmarks.
- Gameplay purpose: teach basic combat, early route choice, relic hunting, the
  first elite, and basic survival pressure.
- Threats: Ashbound, Burdened, Censer, Harpy, and early elite variants.
- Routes and hazards: open ruin paths, cave shortcuts, exposed high paths,
  ash storms, collapsing ruins, and poor visibility.
- Relic bias: basic Sovereign and early survival relics.

## 2. Ward of Irons

- Theme: industrial confinement and machinery without purpose.
- Visual identity: rusted machinery, pipes, suspended platforms, steam vents,
  grinding gears, iron corridors, and vertical shafts.
- Gameplay purpose: introduce confined pressure, hazard awareness, and
  machinery-based traversal.
- Threats: Burdened, Bellworn, Silencers, and armored variants.
- Routes and hazards: corridors, machinery rooms, shafts, maintenance tunnels,
  steam bursts, crushing machinery, moving platforms, and heat zones.
- Relic bias: heavy Sovereign, heat-based, and armor-piercing effects.

## 3. Hanging Slums

- Theme: precarious life above oblivion.
- Visual identity: hanging homes, rope bridges, chain networks, cliffside
  structures, lanterns, broken lifts, and deep drops.
- Gameplay purpose: teach route risk, vertical navigation, ambushes, exploration
  rewards, and optional hidden dwellings.
- Threats: Harpies, Paranoiacs, Chainrunners, and fast variants.
- Routes and hazards: suspended paths, chain crossings, cliff routes, falling
  structures, weak bridges, wind bursts, and alarm escalation.
- Relic bias: mobility support, tracking, and route-discovery relics.

## 4. Sanctuary

- Theme: temporary safety, melancholy, and preparation.
- Visual identity: suspended cathedral, dim candles, tarnished gold, quiet
  pilgrims, broken statues, storage alcoves, and Anchor light.
- Gameplay purpose: provide recovery, preparation, inventory/build decisions,
  NPC interaction, and campaign transition points.
- Threats: none during normal gameplay; changes to that rule require an explicit
  authored story or endgame decision.
- Routes and hazards: a central hub with connected exits, return paths, and
  locked or revealed chambers.
- Relic bias: archive, storage, comparison, and possible Sanctuary-specific
  relics; exact systems remain UNDEFINED.

## 5. Gallery of Wind

- Theme: exposure, height, and insignificance.
- Visual identity: vast chambers, floating ruins, wind streams, broken bridges,
  vertical light shafts, and distant suspended islands.
- Gameplay purpose: expand verticality, open combat, aerial threats, route
  commitment, and wind-based traversal without becoming a movement shooter.
- Threats: Harpies, aerial elites, long-range enemies, and wind-displaced enemies.
- Routes and hazards: exposed paths, floating platforms, wind-assisted routes,
  falling risk, sudden gusts, and visibility shifts.
- Relic bias: Moonchild, anti-air, recoil-control, and airborne effects.

## 6. Mirror-Crust

- Theme: reality distortion, reflection, and uncertainty.
- Visual identity: reflective surfaces, glass terrain, duplicated architecture,
  inverted spaces, pale light, repeated silhouettes, and remembered rooms.
- Gameplay purpose: hidden routes, ambushes, spatial uncertainty, reflection
  secrets, and advanced enemy modifiers while preserving readable rules.
- Threats: Silencers, mirror enemies, altered elites, and ambush variants.
- Routes and hazards: misleading paths, reflected shortcuts, hidden spaces,
  mirror illusions, false floors, gravity distortions, and delayed reflections.
- Relic bias: Omission, Void, reflection, and duplicate-projectile effects.

## 7. The Breach

- Theme: collapse, final ascent, and reality failing to hold its shape.
- Visual identity: fractured geometry, broken sky, floating cathedral pieces,
  impossible angles, void light, split terrain, and reality scars.
- Gameplay purpose: endgame combat, bosses, high-risk traversal, campaign
  completion, major rewards, and optional post-campaign systems.
- Threats: elite variants, bosses, mixed groups, and authored replay variants.
- Routes and hazards: dangerous vertical routes, boss gates, fragmented paths,
  void fractures, geometry collapse, suppression waves, and reality shifts.
- Relic bias: mythic, transcendent, cycle/endgame-exclusive, and build-defining
  named relics; exact availability remains UNDEFINED.

## Strata rule

The Strata are authored places, not interchangeable loot biomes. Their geometry
should remain recognizable while encounters, loot, optional events, and build
possibilities vary through validated systems. Any new Stratum content must
preserve its environmental identity, atmosphere, gameplay purpose, enemy role,
route identity, hazard identity, relic bias, and narrative significance.
