CENOTAPH ENEMY MODEL GENERATION POLICY

STATUS

Automatic enemy model design is disabled.

Coding agents and asset generators must not infer anatomy, clothing, weapons,
ornament, damage, posture, materials, or iconography from an enemy name, role,
behavior tag, lore entry, or gameplay description.

CURRENT RUNTIME RULE

Every enemy placeholder is one neutral primitive. Width, height, depth, tint,
movement, elevation, timing, and HUD treatment may communicate gameplay role.
Compound geometry and production-looking generated silhouettes are forbidden.

The primitive is not canonical art and must not be described as a creature
design. Enemy gameplay roles remain documented in ENEMY_GAMEPLAY_ROSTER.txt,
but that roster does not authorize visual invention.

REPLACEMENT REQUIREMENT

Replace an enemy primitive only after the developer provides at least one of:

- a sketch or paint-over
- a visual reference set
- an authored model
- an explicit written shape and material direction for that specific enemy

Until then, keep the primitive. Silence and honest scaffolding are preferable to
confidently invented art.
