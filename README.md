
Cenotaph

Cenotaph is a personal creative project exploring dreams, nightmares, emotion, imagination, and the limits of human understanding.

This repository is the central home for everything related to Cenotaph, including:

* Worldbuilding, lore, mythology, and narrative ideas
* Creative direction, atmosphere, and visual language
* Game design and gameplay experiments
* Source code, prototypes, and technical research
* Artwork, references, documentation, and abandoned ideas

Principles

* Cenotaph remains authored by its creator.
* Ideas are not commitments to implement them.
* Uncertainty and contradictions may be preserved.
* Experiments do not have to become part of the final game.
Status

The project is exploratory. Its form, technology, and eventual scope remain open.
